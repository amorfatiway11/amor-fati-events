Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Amor Fati Events (“Amor Fati Events,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information provided through our website, inquiry forms, advertising forms, and communications with us.

Information We Collect

When you contact Amor Fati Events, request information, submit an inquiry, or request a quote, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Event date
  • Event location
  • Event type
  • Estimated guest count
  • Services you are interested in
  • Budget or planning information
  • Other event details or information you choose to provide

We may also automatically collect limited technical information when you visit our website, including your browser type, device information, IP address, referring pages, and interactions with our website.

How We Use Your Information

We may use information collected through our website and forms to:

  • Respond to inquiries and quote requests
  • Communicate with you about your event
  • Provide information about our services
  • Prepare proposals, estimates, invoices, or contracts
  • Coordinate services for booked events
  • Provide customer service
  • Improve our website, advertising, and services
  • Maintain appropriate business and administrative records
  • Send promotional or marketing communications where permitted by law and where you have provided any consent required

Lead Forms and Event Inquiries

When you submit an inquiry or lead form, the information you provide is used to respond to your request and communicate with you regarding Amor Fati Events and the services in which you have expressed interest.

Submission of an inquiry does not create a contractual relationship or guarantee availability for a particular event date.

Communications

If you provide your email address or phone number, we may use that information to respond to your inquiry and communicate with you regarding your requested services, proposal, booking, or event.

If you separately consent to receive promotional communications, you may opt out of those communications at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions provided or by contacting us.

Cookies, Analytics and Advertising

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors interact with our website, improve website performance, and measure the effectiveness of our advertising.

We may use third-party analytics and advertising services, including Google services, which may collect information about visits and interactions with our website according to their own privacy practices.

You can control or disable certain cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

Sharing of Information

Amor Fati Events does not sell your personal information.

We may share information with trusted service providers when reasonably necessary to operate our business or provide services to you. These may include website hosting providers, event management platforms, payment processors, communication providers, analytics providers, advertising platforms, and other vendors that support our operations.

We may also disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our legal rights, customers, business, or others.

Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to or integrations with third-party websites and services. Those services operate independently from Amor Fati Events and may have their own privacy policies.

We encourage you to review the privacy practices of third-party services before providing information directly to them.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information provided to us. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide services, maintain business records, fulfill legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect our legitimate business interests.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning certain personal information we maintain about you. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to our practices, services, or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
